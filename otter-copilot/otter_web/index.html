What you're looking at

Each meeting becomes a transcript plus a live topic graph. Green nodes are decisions and action items; pink-bordered nodes are "good points". Click a topic to get a one-paragraph recap.

Who can see what (three access tiers)

  • Owner — you, holding the owner token. You see every meeting (live and past) and all controls.
  • Shared transcript link — a single read-only transcript you hand out. No token, no other meetings.
  • Public / guest — anyone with the URL. Sees only meetings you've explicitly marked public.

Your sign-in link is your key

There's no password — your sign-in link is your access (anyone with it becomes owner, so keep it private). Bookmark it so you can get back in.

Sharing a single meeting

Open a meeting and press share to copy a read-only link to that one transcript. Use the audience card to mark a meeting private / cohort / public.
