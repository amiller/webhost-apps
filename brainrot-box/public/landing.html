the brainrot box

The brainrot box learned to take notes. It follows your meeting through a scoped OAuth3 token — or the on-stage 🎤 mic, a judge reads the transcript over encrypted inference, and when somebody lands a banger the canvas flashes the quote and writes it in a ledger. The meeting gets a score now.

▶  open the box live · your meeting → judged → painted · e2ee  ·  the original: /interleave/app
✓ live — spec'd, swarm-built (PR #84), and deployed the same day

where it came from

Named out loud in a planning call, the day before mock demo day.

Demo day planning, 2026-07-15. The idea on the table: judging shouldn't just be humans with scorecards. Let a model watch the room too, and count what actually lands.

"we can have … some sort of a judge that basically judges … how many good insights? How many bangers there is, right?"— Tina, Demo Day Planning · 2026-07-15
"The good point. Yeah, yeah. The good point detection."— same call, one minute later, the name sticking
"identifying a loop of how something was before, feedback … then making a change, improving it, and then you have some kind of a value marker that … observes the improvement."— Andrew, same call, on what makes work worth showing

That last quote is the reason this exists: the detector is the value marker. Bangers per meeting is a number. Numbers go in ledgers. Ledgers close feedback loops.
