Your screen streams to a pod sink under a scoped, revocable capability that oauth3 signs once — the sink then verifies it offline (cryptographically, anchored on the issuer DID, no core call). This inverts the usual model: instead of delegating read access to a session you hold, you delegate a live outbound stream. Revoke and the sink stops honoring the same capability, live.
