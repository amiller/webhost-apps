screen → pod, under consent

A debug/sample app for streaming your screen INTO a pod app. Capture is the proven getDisplayMedia → downsample → luma → JPEG pipeline. The stream runs under a scoped, revocable consent grant bound to your oauth3 identity — not a raw POST. This inverts the usual oauth3 model: it delegates a live outbound stream of your screen, not read access to a session. Revoke kills it; the next frame 401s, here.
