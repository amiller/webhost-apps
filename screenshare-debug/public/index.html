oauth3 · outbound-stream consent · change detection (#71)

Screenshare Debug

Your screen streams to a pod sink under a scoped, revocable capability oauth3 signs once; the sink verifies it offline. On top of that pipe, cheap pixel math answers "did something change, and where?" per frame — so a still frame skips the wire entirely, a local change sends the frame, and a scene change (alt-tab, page nav) flags the whole frame. A hi-res keyframe can be pulled on demand — the hook OCR and a visual model use when tile-diff alone is not enough.
