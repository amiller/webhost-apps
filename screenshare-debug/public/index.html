share your screen with the devs

Hit a bug? Start, share one window (the picker scopes the capture — devs see only that window, never your screen), reproduce it, Stop. Frames stay in this browser until you review them and press Upload: delete what you don't want, black out what you must — blackout is applied before the JPEG is encoded, so redacted pixels never exist as an image. Near-identical frames are dropped automatically (smaller traces). Unkept traces expire on the sink after 1 hour.

the mechanism
record: getDisplayMedia → downsample → luma → change-drop → in-page canvas store (zero network)
review: filmstrip · per-frame delete · blackout rects in frame-pixel coords · freeform note
upload: rects filled black → JPEG encode → POST sink/frame/<sid>/<seq> · session meta POSTed first
sink: deno, zero deps · sessions under .data/sessions/<sid>/ · 1h TTL unless kept
synthetic: ?synthetic=1 — animated canvas source, no capture permission needed
