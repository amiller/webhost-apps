What you've delegated

A different mechanism from identity. With the OAuth3 extension, every site you're already logged into becomes a login provider: the app receives a scoped read token for that one site — revocable, never your cookies. A card with no jar yet is a cold start: connect your browser to sync, not a dead-end.
